A card game about what we keep human

Human-Focused Design Card Game

2 to 6 players, best with 4 · 15 to 20 minutes a round · You also need a pen and paper

13 Scenario cards
A magical world where a robot or AI helps.
13 Dilemma cards + 2 blank
A problem with no easy answer.
13 Tool cards
A question to look at the problem through.
13 Twist cards
A rule that makes the choice harder.

How to play

1
Draw a world

Turn over a Scenario card and read it aloud.

2
Draw a problem

Turn over a Dilemma card. Imagine it happening in this world.

3
Add a twist

Turn over a Twist card. It changes the rules for this round.

4
Pick your lens

Deal two Tool cards to each player, face down. Each player keeps one in secret.

5
Reveal and discuss

Everyone shows their card. Find two cards that disagree, and talk about why.

6
Decide together

Write down: our decision, what the robot will do, what people will still do, and what we gave up.

7
Reflect

Who might feel most uncomfortable with this decision? Did the robot skip anything people should do themselves?

Conditions, not answers.

Print sheets 1 to 6 double-sided, flipping on the long edge. Cut along the dashed lines. Each card is about 62 × 87 mm.
